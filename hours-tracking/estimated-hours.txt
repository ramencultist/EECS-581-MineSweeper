Hours Estimation Methodology:
To estimate hours we used the planned poker method to give an estimate of the effort of each of the modules. To convert
this to an estimate of person-hours as required by grading criteria, we applied a productivity rate to the estimates.
We agreed upon an estimate number of .75 hours per effort point. For example an effort of 8 would be 6 person hours. Our
estimates of difficulty included documentation and testing.

As noted in the materials from Professor Saiedian, the estimate of hours is likely to be wrong, however we endeavored
to create a clear and well-justified estimate. Estimates could be proven wrong from difficulties that arise from
uncertainty.
